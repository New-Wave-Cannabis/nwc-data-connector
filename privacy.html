Privacy Policy

New Wave Cannabis data connector for QuickBooks Online
Effective date: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how New Wave Cannabis (“NWC,” “we,” or “us”) handles information received through the New Wave Cannabis data connector for QuickBooks Online (the “Connector”). It applies to the companies that connect the Connector and to the people who authorize it.

1. Information we receive

  • Accounting records of the connected QuickBooks Online company, such as vendors, accounts, bills, bill payments, vendor credits, and expenses. These records can include names, addresses, and contact details of the company’s vendors and other business contacts.
  • Company identifiers, such as the QuickBooks company ID and company name.
  • Authorization tokens that Intuit issues when the Connector is authorized.

We do not receive the QuickBooks username or password of the person who authorizes the Connector. These pages use no cookies, analytics, or tracking.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to provide the reporting, reconciliation, and data services that the connected company has engaged NWC to provide, and to keep the connection working (for example, to detect a failed authorization).

3. Read-only access

The Connector reads records; it does not create, change, or delete records in QuickBooks Online.

4. How we store and protect it

  • Authorization tokens are kept in the operating system’s protected credential store on NWC-controlled computers. They are never written to source code, shared documents, or logs.
  • Accounting records are kept in an NWC-managed database that limits each user to the organizations they are permitted to see.
  • Access is limited to NWC personnel and systems that need it to provide the services.

5. Sharing

We do not sell this information or share it for advertising. We share it only with the connected company and the people it authorizes; with service providers that host our systems, under confidentiality obligations; or when the law requires it.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep the information for as long as the service engagement continues and as needed to meet legal and accounting obligations. When the engagement ends, or on the connected company’s written request, we delete the authorization tokens and delete or return the accounting records, unless the law requires us to keep them.

7. Your choices

The connected company may disconnect the Connector at any time in QuickBooks Online, which stops all further access, and may ask us to delete the information we hold.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the current version. If a change materially affects a connected company, we will tell it before the change takes effect.

9. Contact
